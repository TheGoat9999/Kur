PRODUCT NORTH STAR

Persistent urban RPG, който генерира истории чрез взаимосвързани системи.

SOL DORADO не цели да копира FiveM feature count. Целта е да вземе най-силното от RP сървърите — икономика, престъпност, careers, businesses, полиция, имоти, social systems — и да го превърне в world-first browser/mobile gameplay.

Target MVP

MVP доказва дали urban RP работи като browser/mobile game без GTA gameplay.

Closed Alpha target

Какво влиза в MVP

Достатъчно breadth, за да има град, но достатъчно depth, за да се пресичат системите.

WorldSol Dorado City, 5–6 zones, ~15–20 districts, streets, NPC population, events, global time.
CharacterCreator, basic hair/clothes, needs, inventory, phone, identity, persistence.
EconomyCash, bank, shops, item economy, loans, earning/spending loops.
Work~5 entry activities + Police + Mechanic + Real Estate + one business vertical.
VehiclesBuy/sell, parking, fuel, damage, trunk, theft, travel.
PropertyRent/buy, home, storage, parking, security, commercial property.
Crime8–10 linked activities from petty crime to one advanced robbery.
PoliceNPC + player police, witnesses, evidence, dispatch, arrest, jail, warrants.
SocialPlayer encounters, contacts, trust, messaging, crews, simple gangs.
